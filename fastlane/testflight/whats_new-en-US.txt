Welcome to the first test version of Fit mit Jodit!

Please try everything once:
1. First launch: read the welcome screen. If it says only the basic voice is installed, download a Premium voice as described (e.g. "Ava (Premium)"), then tap "Preview voice".
2. Templates: "Circuit 45/15", "Tabata", "Strength", "Warm-up", "Pyramid" (for class, no exercise names) plus "Full Body 20", "Core 10", "Legs & Glutes 15", "Cardio 12", "Upper Body 12", "EMOM 12" and "Stretch 10" (for home, with voice cues).
3. Start a workout: 10 s lead-in (blue), then work (green) and rest (orange). Tap the screen once: pause, skip back/forward, stop.
4. Voice cues: start "Full Body 20" – the voice announces exercise and duration, "Get ready" and "10 seconds left". In Settings: cues Off/Automatic/Always, choose a voice.
5. Build your own workout: "+" at the top, tap blocks at the bottom (e.g. 3× 45 s work with 15 s rest, 1 min rest), tap blocks to select them, "× N" for rounds. Pick an exercise, change duration, duplicate, delete, move, undo.
6. Tones: three tone sets in Settings. With Spotify in the background: does the music get quieter during the tones?
7. iPad: put Spotify next to it in Split View – is the timer still readable?
8. Lock Screen: lock the device during a workout – is the countdown visible?
9. Spotify link: in a workout use "…" › Spotify link to paste a playlist, then "Open in Spotify".
10. Apple Watch (if you have one): open the app on the watch, start a workout on the iPhone, control it from the watch.
11. At the end: finish screen with a link to fitmitjodit.ch and "Share Workout".
12. Lock: tap during a workout › lock. The screen then ignores taps; press and hold to unlock. The watch keeps working.
13. QR code: QR icon at the top of a workout. Scan with a second iPhone (app installed) using the camera – the workout is added.
14. History: clock icon at the top left of the workout list – finished workouts and weekly overview.
15. Siri: "Start Tabata with Fit mit Jodit", "Pause Fit mit Jodit", "Next block in Fit mit Jodit".
16. Resume: start a workout, swipe the app away in the app switcher, open it again – the workout continues at the right point.
17. Tones: the 3-2-1 countdown is softer and no longer distorts. Does it sound clean on your device, also with Spotify in the background?
18. Moving blocks (new in this build): touch and hold a block in the builder, then drag. You don't have to hit a target exactly, a bar shows where it will land. Into a round, out of a round, or grab a whole round by its header. On the 11-inch iPad the blocks palette now sits on the right.
19. Dark mode (new in this build): buttons and icons are now light and no longer look inactive, "Start" is a light button with dark text.
20. Remote control (new in this build): the "Allow remote control?" question on the iPad now also appears over the welcome screen and during a workout, without anything closing by itself.

Feedback: take a screenshot in TestFlight › "Send Feedback", or email fitmitjodit@scheuber.me.
